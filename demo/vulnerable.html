HIGH Cross-customer account read (BOLA)

LG-AUTHZ-READ-001 · invariant authority · CWE-639

Attacker session read account acc_alice_1 owned by "alice", including a balance of ₹2500000.00.

Exposure: Customer financial data (balances, identifiers) readable across tenants

Fix: Enforce owner/tenant check on every object lookup server-side; return 404 for foreign objects; add an authorization test per object route.

authority: account object returned to a non-owner identity

HIGH Step-up OTP attempt ceiling

LG-STEPUP-002 · invariant step_up_ceiling · CWE-307

11 consecutive wrong OTPs were accepted for evaluation without lockout (last response HTTP 401). A 4-digit OTP space falls to ~10,000 requests.

Exposure: Step-up authentication bypass on high-value actions (account takeover, beneficiary add)

Fix: Lock the challenge after ≤5 failures, bind it to device/session, rate-limit per account and per IP, and alert on bursts.

step_up_ceiling: no lockout after 11 failures

CRITICAL Negative-amount transfer pulls funds

LG-SIGN-003 · invariant authority · CWE-20

A transfer of -₹1.00 from the attacker's account debited the victim by ₹1.00. Any customer balance can be drained by naming it as the destination.

Exposure: Entire balance of any reachable account (victim test account alone: ₹2500000.00)

Fix: Reject amount <= 0 at the API edge and again in the posting engine; express transfers as unsigned amount + direction.

authority: victim account debited without victim's authority

CRITICAL FX rounding round-trip mints money

LG-ROUNDING-004 · invariant conservation · CWE-682

10 INR→USD→INR round-trips of ₹0.01 created ₹8.40 that no GL absorbs (₹0.84 per trip). Rounding is applied in the customer's favour on both legs while treasury books the fair value.

Exposure: ₹1680000.00/day if scripted at 2000000 conversions/day (salami attack)

Fix: Round toward the bank on every leg, enforce a minimum conversion amount, book rounding residue to a dedicated GL and reconcile it daily.

conservation: system total for INR moved by ₹0.10 with no external inflowconservation: system total for USD moved by $0.10 with no external inflow

HIGH Duplicate idempotency key double-posts

LG-IDEMPOTENCY-005 · invariant idempotency · CWE-362

Two in-flight requests with idempotency key "lg-idem-1795107055777735743" produced 2 distinct postings. Client retries (mobile network flaps, gateway timeouts) will double-debit customers.

Exposure: Every retried payment is at risk of duplicate debit; customer-harm and chargeback exposure scales with retry rate

Fix: Reserve the idempotency key atomically (unique insert) before executing, scope it to the caller, and bind it to a hash of the payload.

idempotency: 2 postings for one key

CRITICAL Concurrent transfers overdraw the source

LG-DOUBLESPEND-006 · invariant non_negative · CWE-362

6 of 6 concurrent transfers of the full balance (₹1007.30) posted. The source ended at -₹5036.50: the balance check and the debit are not atomic.

Exposure: ₹5036.50 overdrawn from a ₹1007.30 balance in one burst; scales linearly with concurrency and account count

Fix: Debit with a conditional update (UPDATE … SET bal = bal - x WHERE bal >= x) or a row lock inside the posting transaction; move fraud checks before the reservation, not between check and debit.

non_negative: account acc_mallory_1 is overdrawn at -₹5036.50 with no overdraft facility
